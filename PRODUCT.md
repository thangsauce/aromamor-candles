# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Aromamor serves shoppers looking for handmade candles that feel personal, giftable, and more meaningful than mass-market home fragrance. The primary buyer wants a candle with visible care, a story, and enough polish to give as a thoughtful gift. Secondary buyers are travel-inspired and self-care shoppers who want a scent tied to a place, memory, or mood.

## Product Purpose

Aromamor is a handmade candle storefront built around destination-inspired scents. The site should make the collection feel transportive and crafted by hand while keeping the path to browse, save, and buy candles clear. Success means visitors quickly understand that these are small-batch handmade candles, remember the travel story, and feel confident choosing a scent for themselves or as a gift.

## Brand Personality

Warm, transportive, refined. Aromamor should feel like a handmade luxury travel boutique: personal and human, but not casual or craft-fair. The voice should make scent feel like a passport, with enough restraint to stay premium and enough warmth to feel approachable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic Shopify candle stores, childish craft-fair visuals, overly beige luxury branding, dark moody perfume sites, and corporate wellness language. The site should not look mass-produced, sterile, trend-chasing, or like a template product grid with candle photos dropped in.

## Design Principles

Show the hand behind the product.

Make every scent feel like a place.

Lead with feeling, then make shopping easy.

Use polish without removing warmth.

Let destination color break up the neutral base.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Keep product browsing usable on mobile, maintain clear focus states for forms and buttons, avoid relying on color alone for product status, and respect reduced-motion preferences for any animation.
