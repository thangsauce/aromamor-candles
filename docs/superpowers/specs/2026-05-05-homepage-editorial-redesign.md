# Homepage Editorial Redesign

## Goal

Redesign the Aromamor home page so it feels closer to the provided reference in structure, pacing, and editorial rhythm, while staying true to Aromamor's warmer candle-and-travel identity.

This is not a fashion-brand copy. The redesign should borrow the long-scroll composition, image-led storytelling, and spacious hierarchy, but keep Aromamor's own voice, product context, and emotional tone.

## Direction

Use the reference for:
- editorial pacing
- quieter, more intentional section rhythm
- stronger image-led moments
- larger statement typography
- more breathing room between content groups

Do not copy:
- fashion-brand personality
- category naming logic
- overly delicate or distant tone
- clothing-editorial presentation conventions that do not fit candles

## Core Experience

The home page should feel less like stacked storefront blocks and more like a curated story that leads naturally into discovery.

The page should:
- open with one strong hero image and restrained copy
- transition into a large mission statement band
- present the collection more selectively and beautifully
- add at least one image-led story section in the middle
- maintain a clear path to shop without feeling grid-first

## Section Plan

## 1. Hero

Purpose:
- Keep the large immersive first impression
- Feel more editorial and less conventionally e-commerce

Layout:
- Full-screen image hero
- Left-aligned text block with more restraint
- Cleaner CTA treatment
- Softer hierarchy than the current home hero

Content direction:
- Keep the current candle-memory/travel emotional framing
- Avoid turning the hero into a loud sales panel

## 2. Mission Statement Band

Purpose:
- Replace the current standard mission section feel with a more declarative editorial moment

Layout:
- Wide statement-led section
- Small eyebrow on the left
- Larger statement copy with more presence
- Supporting text treated more lightly

## 3. Selective Product Discovery

Purpose:
- Keep shopping access clear while reducing visual density

Layout:
- Fewer, larger product moments
- More negative space
- Product presentation feels curated instead of crowded

Content direction:
- Highlight the collection as destinations and moods, not only products

## 4. Story Image Section

Purpose:
- Add one strong visual pause mid-page
- Make the home page feel more editorial and less transactional

Layout:
- One larger image-led section
- Short supporting text or caption
- Strong spacing above and below

## 5. Secondary Discovery / Editorial Bridge

Purpose:
- Bridge story and shopping
- Keep the page flowing rather than breaking into unrelated chunks

Layout:
- Can combine image and text asymmetrically
- Should feel quieter and more composed than the current layout

## 6. Reviews / Trust Section

Purpose:
- Retain social proof
- Make it feel integrated into the editorial flow

Layout:
- More breathing room
- Cleaner typography
- Less boxed or abrupt presentation

## 7. Footer Handoff

Purpose:
- Ensure the home page blends into the footer image system smoothly

Layout:
- Background and section-end color must hand off cleanly into the existing footer blend treatment

## Visual Style

- Keep Aromamor's serif display language
- Use larger margins and more whitespace
- Make section breaks feel intentional
- Favor restrained, premium text density
- Use imagery as structure, not decoration

## Brand Guardrails

- Warm, transportive, refined
- Handmade luxury candle brand, not fashion label
- Personal and intimate, not distant or austere
- Premium, but never sterile

## Responsive Behavior

- Mobile should preserve the editorial pacing
- Major image moments should still read clearly on small screens
- Product/discovery sections may stack, but should keep hierarchy and calm spacing

## Success Criteria

- Home page feels clearly more editorial than before
- Still unmistakably Aromamor
- Shopping remains easy to find
- Page feels curated, spacious, and intentional
- Reference influence is visible in rhythm and composition, not copied literally
